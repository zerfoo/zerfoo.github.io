01 / THE IDEA

A model should fit your world.
Not the other way around.

Start with a problem worth solving. Zerfoo brings model design and a Go framework together, so you can move toward a working model without handing over your data or your compute.

01 — DESIGN

Give your idea a shape.

Talk through your task, data and hardware. Inspect the proposed layers in the model viewer, then download the project.

Open the designer ↗
02 — BUILD & TRAIN

Do the work locally.

Use the framework directly, or bring your coding agent and Kazi. Review the code, train on your compute and evaluate against a clear goal.

Find your starting point ↗
03 — RUN

Make it part of your work.

Verify saved models in a fresh process. Embed supported inference in your Go application or serve it through an OpenAI-compatible API.

Run your first inference ↗

The designer produces project files, not trained weights. Numeric classification has a verified starter; other architectures need local engineering and validation.

03 / A LONG-RUNNING QUESTION

What if building AI
felt like building software?

I’m David Ndungu. In 2019, I started Therfoo to explore neural networks in Go, from dense layers and activation functions to backpropagation. Zerfoo carries that work forward with a broader foundation for creating and running models.

The goal is still personal: make the machinery understandable, keep the tools open, and help more people turn an idea into something they can test. There is more to build. You can follow the work, inspect the decisions, and build alongside me.
